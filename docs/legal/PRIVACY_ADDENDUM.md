## Akshara World — Privacy Addendum for Launch Pilot Customers

*This section supplements the existing Privacy Policy.*

---

## Data we collect for Launch Pilot customers

| Data | Purpose | Storage |
|------|---------|---------| 
| Name, email, payment ID | Account, receipt, support | Google Sheets `Transactions`, Clerk |
| Google account (OAuth) | Dashboard access, Sheets SOT | Clerk, Google APIs |
| Chat messages | Customer support | Google Chat |
| Usage analytics | Product improvement | Google Analytics 4 |
| Sam directive logs | Operations audit | Google Sheets `SystemLog` |

## Retention and deletion

- **Transactions and SystemLog:** retained in Google Sheets until you request deletion.
- **Deletion requests:** contact us via the support channel provided at purchase. We will delete or anonymize Sheets rows tied to your account within thirty (30) days.
- **Backups:** Google Drive sync and GitHub code backups may retain data per Google/GitHub retention policies.

## Third-party processors

Cloudflare (hosting), Razorpay (payments), Clerk (auth), Google (Sheets, Drive, GA4, Gmail), GitHub (code), Telegram (operational alerts). Full vendor list maintained in Guardian_Ops folder.

## Your rights (India / GDPR-aligned practices)

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we control in Sheets and Clerk. Payment records held by Razorpay are subject to Razorpay's privacy policy.
