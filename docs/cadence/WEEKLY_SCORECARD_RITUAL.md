# Weekly Scorecard Ritual (~60 min, Sunday)

## 1. Revenue vs target (15 min)

- Add row to `WeeklyScorecard` for week ending today
- Fields: touches, demos, early_bird_sold, standard_sold, revenue_inr, refunds_inr, net_revenue_inr
- Pipeline counts: contacted, replied, demo, paid (from `SalesPipeline`)

## 2. Funnel by stage (10 min)

- Which channel has best contacted → paid ratio?
- Note in `notes` for Week 4 channel focus

## 3. Content (10 min)

- Schedule one post from `docs/content/content-calendar-week3-4.md` for coming week

## 4. Sam review (10 min)

- Which department directives led to revenue or pipeline movement?
- Adjust priorities in `sam-brain/ops-config.json` if needed

## 5. Backup (10 min)

- Confirm Google Drive synced Sheets SOT
- `git push origin main` for AksharaWorld repo

## 6. Looker (5 min, optional)

- Open Looker Studio dashboard
- Screenshot for records

## 7. Digest (optional)

- Send weekly digest email to list if Week ≥ 4
